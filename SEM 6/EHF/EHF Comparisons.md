## Differentiate White, Black and Grey Hat Hackers
Factor | White Hat | Black Hat | Grey Hat
---|---|---|---
Legal permission | Has permission | No permission | Mixed permission
Intent | Improve security | Personal gain | Notify owner
Methods | Legal methods | Illegal methods | Both legal/illegal
Disclosure | Reports findings | Hides findings | May disclose
Motivation | Ethical defense | Malicious attack | Curiosity/fun
Penalty risk | No penalty | Heavy penalty | Occasional penalty
Example | Certified tester | Data thief | Hacks then informs
Law alignment | Fully legal | Fully illegal | Between laws

## Compare active attacks vs Passive attacks
| Factor          | Active Attacks      | Passive Attacks     |
| --------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| Goal            | Modify system       | Monitor/steal data  |
| Detection       | Easy to detect      | Hard to detect      |
| System impact   | Disrupts operations | No disruption       |
| Example         | DoS, spoofing       | Sniffing, eavesdrop |
| Data alteration | Yes                 | No                  |
| Resource use    | High                | Low                 |
| Countermeasure  | Firewalls, IDS      | Encryption          |
| Legality        | Clearly illegal     | Gray area           |

Factor | Hackers | Crackers | Phreakers
---|---|---|---
Primary focus | Systems/networks | Breaking software | Phone networks

Ethics | Often ethical | Always unethical | Mixed ethics
Skill level | High programming | High programming | Telecom specialist

## Difference between hacking and ethical hacking with examples
Factor | Hacking | Ethical Hacking
---|---|---
Permission | Unauthorized | Authorized
Legality | Illegal | Legal
Intent | Malicious/curious | Defensive/improve
Reporting | No report | Full report prepared
Example 1 | Stealing credit cards | Bank security test
Example 2 | Defacing website | Hospital penetration test
Example 3 | Installing ransomware | Government system audit
Outcome | Harm/loss | Security fix

## Differentiate between Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing
Factor | Vulnerability Assessment | Penetration Testing
---|---|---
Objective | List weaknesses | Exploit weaknesses
Depth | Surface scan | Deep exploitation
Automation | Mostly automated | Mostly manual
Report type | Vulnerability list | Proof-of-concept attacks
False positives | Common | Rare
Time required | Hours to days | Days to weeks
Risk to system | Very low | Moderate risk
Final result | Prioritized vulnerabilities | Actual breach paths
