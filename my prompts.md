If question is of differentiation then the format will be:
    > Answer each question strictly in a table. Each cell uses 2-6 words only.
    > No introductions, summaries, or remarks before/after the table.
    > Minimum 8 Points, Maximum 16 points
    >
    > **Format:**
    > Factor | Topic1 | Topic2 | ... | TopicN

If question consists of "Explain X, Y, Z, ..." then the format will  be (always if more than 2 topics):
    X (first explain/define in a sentence of 12 words):
        > (definition) sentence 1
        > (if needed) sentence 2
        > (if needed) sentence 3
    Each of sentence of 8-12 words
    Y (first explain/define in a sentence of 12 words):
        > (definition) sentence 1
        > (if needed) sentence 2
        > (if needed) sentence 3

    Z (first explain/define in a sentence of 12 words):
        > (definition) sentence 1
        > (if needed) sentence 2
        > (if needed) sentence 3
    Each of sentence of 8-12 words
    ...
